model call, hashes in `FREEZE_PRE.txt`) and `REPORT.md` with the numbers and limits. This file is the map.

## E1: how many past conversations the probe generator needs

Spearman between the probe failure rate and the real failure rate over the 50 TauIndianBank families (6 probes and 6 real
tasks per family, bootstrap over families):

| generator saw | gemini-3.8-flash | gemini-2.5-flash | Claude Haiku | Claude Sonnet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| documentation only (k0) | 0.31 | 0.53 | 0.48 | 0.45 |
| 2 past conversations of the family (k2) | 0.70 | 0.73 | 0.65 | 0.73 |
| 5 past conversations (k5) | 0.75 | 0.80 | 0.79 | 0.73 |
| 2 conversations of another family (foreign) | 0.40 | 0.47 | 0.30 | 0.53 |

- k5 beats documentation probes for every agent (paired interval above 0).
- From 2 to 5 conversations nothing significant is added for any agent.
- Conversations of another family never beat documentation probes. k2 beats foreign with the interval above 0 for
  gemini-3.8-flash and Claude Haiku, and touches 0 for the other two. So the gain comes from seeing this procedure's own
  scenario. TauIndianBank families are templated, so a "past conversation" is nearly the test with another customer, and
  the k2 and k5 numbers are an upper bound for real past tickets.
- With k5, families where all 6 probes passed had 0.90 to 0.95 real success for all four agents.

The Claude runs use Claude Haiku as the customer for real tasks and probes alike, so they are compared within Claude,
not with the Gemini numbers. Agents are fresh `claude -p` sessions that act only through `claude_tib/bridge.py`
(tau2 environment and replay); scoring is tau2's `evaluate_simulation`, as for Gemini.

## E4: the diagnostic test on fixed SOPBench labels

`aar_diag/diag.py` reproduced exactly on the published labels, then rerun with labels from the fixed grader
(`data/sopbench_fixed`) and with Sonnet as a second predictor. AUC: case text only stays at chance (0.50 to 0.51 for
both predictors and both label sets). Sonnet gains from the procedure name (0.58 vs 0.51, Brier skill difference above
0), Haiku does not. With the procedure's own counts Sonnet reaches the no-LLM count (0.66), Haiku stays below and its
calibration changed a lot between runs. Counting without an LLM is best or tied in all four runs.

This concerns a zero-shot reader of one case. Supervised documentation features (c_lad, f2) rank procedures at
rho 0.68-0.72 on the fixed labels (`data/sopbench_fixed/CLAD_F2_REPORT.md`), so "text does not predict" needs that
qualifier.

## E5: text similarity without the family prefix

bge-base-en-v1.5 over each family's purpose line (TauIndianBank has no per-procedure policy text) instead of TF-IDF
with the family-name prefix token. Text kNN with 0 probes becomes worse than the constant (Brier 0.244 to 0.268 against
0.225 to 0.233). "Seeded probes + Memory" qualifies at 0.90 in 0 to 2% of splits by Clopper-Pearson instead of 1 to 14%
with the prefix, so a good part of its earlier advantage came from the prefix. The Gemini-embedding robustness check
was not run.

## Data in this branch

- `seedk/`, `sim2/`, `claude_tib/`: selections, generated probes (`seedk/probes/<condition>/<family>.json`), one line
  per episode in `runs/*.jsonl`, full trajectories in `runs/trajectories_*.tar.gz`, analysis outputs, ledger.
- `aar_diag_c/`: predictions of every run (`preds_<model>_<labels>.jsonl`) and scores.
- `e5_embed/`: embeddings (`emb_tib.npz`) and the three similarity runs.
- `data/sopbench_fixed/`: the repaired SOPBench (806 cases, grader fixes, task edits), re-scored labels of the 28
  published agents, our runs on it (`runs.tar.gz`) and reports. The code that built it is not in this branch.
- `sopaudit_runtime/`: the analysis of "which request fails" and of a commit-time check on SOPBench-fixed (the check
  before a write action that the handoff mentions as Asanali's parallel task), numbers only.

## Deviations from the handoff

- E1 k1 covers 28 families and E2 a third of its episodes: the 70 USD total.
- E4 uses Claude predictors, not Gemini.
- E5 used the existing baseline venv (sentence-transformers 6.1.0, torch 2.14.0, numpy 2.4.6), not the pinned list.
- The scripts were copied from the working tree with absolute paths replaced by relative ones, so the hashes in the
  `FREEZE_PRE.txt` files refer to the pre-copy versions. Shell runners take the tau2 python from `TAU_PY`.
- The Mac slept for 25 minutes during the E1 pilot. Three Gemini runners were restarted and finished episodes were kept.
  A stalled simulator call stopped the Claude Sonnet k0 runner once. The bridge now retries such calls, and the
  remaining episodes were run.
